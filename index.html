Questions founders ask first

Short answers here, longer ones in Insights. Ask us anything else on the intro call.

How much does a startup lawyer cost in Dubai?

We work on fixed fees, monthly retainers or milestone billing instead of hourly rates, and for selected ventures can take part of the fee in equity. Ask for a quote before you commit to anyone.

Can a UAE startup raise on a SAFE?

Yes. SAFEs are widely used by UAE and MENA startups, usually through a holding company in ADGM, DIFC or an offshore jurisdiction. The right structure depends on where your investors sit and where you plan to raise next.

What is a fractional general counsel?

A senior lawyer who acts as your in-house counsel for a set number of hours or matters each month, handling contracts, governance and board matters without the cost of a full-time hire.

Do you work with investors as well as founders?

Yes. We have sat on both sides of the table and act for angels, syndicates and funds on term sheets, SHAs and portfolio matters.

Which countries do you cover?

The UAE first, Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC regularly, and cross-border matters with partner firms elsewhere. We work in English, Arabic, Spanish, French and Portuguese.
